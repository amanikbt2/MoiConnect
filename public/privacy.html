1. Introduction & Developer Identity

Welcome to MConnect ("we", "our", or "us"), an academic resource sharing, student housing connection, and campus collaboration application developed for the university student community. We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring your personal information is protected in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable data protection regulations (including the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and GDPR standards).

This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, why we collect it, how sensitive device permissions are utilized, our strict data sharing policies, and the simple mechanisms provided to manage or permanently delete your account and personal data.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal and usage data necessary to deliver core platform functionality:

  • Account Credentials & Profile Details: Full name, student or landlord email address, phone number (used for rental house inquiry contact), role selection (Student or Landlord), and secure cryptographically hashed passwords.
  • Google Sign-In Profile Data: When you choose to authenticate via Google Sign-In, we receive your Google account email address, verified display name, and avatar image link provided by Google Identity Services to streamline authentication.
  • Academic Resources & Study Content: Course codes, unit names, academic year, and user-submitted revision past papers or Continuous Assessment Tests (CATs) uploaded to assist campus peers.
  • Rental Housing & Booking Data: Hostel booking inquiries, housing preferences, landlord property listings (room details, amenities, pricing, property photos), and verification records.
  • In-App Messages & Community Discussion: Direct messaging between students and verified landlords, and public campus community discussion posts.
  • Technical & Diagnostics Data: Network connection state, device model, operating system version, and anonymous crash telemetry used exclusively to maintain stability and performance.

4. How We Use Collected Information

We process your information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Authenticating accounts, preventing fraudulent signups, and maintaining platform security.
  • Enabling students to search, view, and download academic past papers and revision notes.
  • Connecting students with verified hostel landlords and facilitating direct property inquiries.
  • Delivering real-time voice and video communication and instant messaging.
  • Distributing push notifications for important messages and incoming calls.

5. Third-Party SDKs & Service Providers

To deliver real-time features and secure infrastructure, MConnect integrates reputable third-party service providers. Each provider processes only the data strictly necessary for their service:

  • Google Identity & Play Services: Used for secure Google Sign-In authentication and base mobile services.
  • LiveKit Cloud (WebRTC): Powers real-time peer-to-peer and relayed audio and video calling. Call media is ephemeral and end-to-end encrypted in transit.
  • Cloudinary: Provides secure cloud storage for user-uploaded profile avatars, document assets, and landlord house photos.
  • Socket.io: Powers real-time chat messaging, presence indicators, and call signaling.
  • Expo Application Services (EAS): Manages application packaging and secure update delivery.

We do not allow any third-party SDK partner to access or utilize student data for cross-service profiling, advertising, or marketing.

6. Zero Sale of Personal Data

MConnect does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information with data brokers, advertisers, or third-party marketing companies. We do not participate in cross-app behavioral tracking or serve personalized targeted advertisements.

7. Data Security Practices

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical security measures to protect user data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using standard bcrypt algorithms before database storage.
  • Stateless Token Authentication: User sessions are authorized using secure JSON Web Tokens (JWT) stored in protected device storage.

8. Account Deletion & User Data Rights (Google Play Standard)

In strict compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, users have the complete right to permanently delete their account and associated personal data at any time. We provide two easy methods:

Method 1: In-App Account Deletion (Instant)

  1. Open the MConnect app and sign in.
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap the three dots (⋮) menu in the top-right corner.
  4. Select "Delete account".
  5. Confirm your decision. Your account and personal records are instantly and permanently purged from our database.

Method 2: Web & Email Deletion Request (For users without app access)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, send an email to amanikbt1@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email address. Requests are validated and permanently executed within 5 business days.

What Data is Permanently Deleted:

  • Personal identity details (Full Name, Email address, Phone number, and Password hashes).
  • User profile avatar images stored on cloud servers.
  • Landlord property listings, rooms, pricing, and uploaded property images.
  • Private direct chat messages, call records, and conversation history.
  • Hostel booking requests, inquiries, and saved favorites.
  • Push notification device tokens.

Data Retention / Community Anonymization:

Academic revision documents (past papers and CAT notes) that were uploaded for public student study access remain available to the campus community to maintain academic continuity; however, all personal author identifiers are permanently detached and anonymized so that the content is completely disassociated from your identity.

9. Children's Privacy (Under 13 / Minors)

MConnect is designed and intended strictly for higher education university and college students (typically aged 18 and older, and no younger than 13). We do not knowingly collect, request, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that personal data from a child under 13 has been collected, we will take prompt steps to immediately purge such information from our records.

10. Regulatory Compliance & User Rights

Depending on your location, you hold the following statutory privacy rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct or update your personal information directly within your Profile screen.
  • Right to Erasure: You can delete your account and data at any time as detailed in Section 8.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request an export of your personal information in a readable format.
