Privacy Policy & Account Deletion

Effective Date: September 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MoiConnect ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data collected through our mobile app and platform services. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and user data deletion rights.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver student academic resource sharing, host housing connections, and campus messaging:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, student/landlord email address, phone number, and encrypted password.
  • Academic Resources: User-uploaded past papers, CAT revision notes, course codes, and unit details.
  • Housing & Rental Requests: Hostel booking inquiries, house preferences, and landlord verification documents (ID numbers).
  • In-App Messages: Messages exchanged between students and verified landlords.

3. How We Use Information

Your information is processed strictly for providing core app functionalities:

  • Facilitating academic revision material downloads and uploads.
  • Connecting students with verified hostel landlords.
  • Authenticating users, maintaining account security, and preventing fraud.
  • Enabling student-to-landlord community communications.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do NOT sell, trade, or share your personal information with third-party advertisers. All data is transmitted securely over TLS/HTTPS encryption.

6. Account Deletion & User Data Rights

You have the absolute right to request the complete deletion of your MoiConnect account and all associated personal data at any time.

  • How to Submit an Account Deletion Request: Send an email to amanikbt1@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" using your registered email address.
  • Data Deleted Upon Request: Account credentials, user profile details (Name, Email, Phone Number), profile pictures, landlord verification documents, saved bookmarks, and private messaging history.
  • Data Retention Policy: Public academic past papers/notes uploaded for student community access will be anonymized or deleted upon explicit request.
  • Processing Timeframe: Account deletion requests are verified and completed within 5 business days.
