🗂️ You Control Your Data

MConnect allows you to delete specific data — such as downloaded files, saved bookmarks, property listings, profile details, or uploaded documents — without closing your account. You can also request full account deletion at any time. All requests are honoured within 5 business days.

🗑️ Want to Delete Your Entire Account Instead?

To permanently close your MConnect account and erase all associated data, visit our Account Deletion FAQ page or use the in-app option: Profile → ⋮ → Delete account.

1. Delete Specific Data Yourself — In-App (Instant)
Self-Service · No Email Required

What can I delete directly inside the MConnect app?

The following data types can be removed immediately without contacting us:

1
Downloaded Academic Files Go to the Downloads tab → tap the options menu (⋮) on any file → select "Delete". The file is immediately removed from your device storage.
2
Saved Bookmarks & Favourites Tap the bookmark or heart icon on any saved paper, hostel, or resource to immediately un-save and remove it from your favourites list.
3
Property Listings (Landlords) Open the Listings Management screen → select a listing → tap "Delete Listing". All photos and pricing details associated with that listing are permanently removed.
4
Profile Personal Information Go to the Profile tab → tap "Edit Profile" to update or clear your display name, phone number, and academic year at any time.
5
Profile Avatar Photo Go to the Profile tab → tap your profile picture → select "Remove Photo". The image is permanently deleted from our cloud storage (Cloudinary).
2. Request Deletion of Specific Data via Email
Email Request · Processed Within 5 Business Days

How do I request removal of specific data that I cannot delete myself?

For data types not covered by in-app self-service (e.g., an uploaded academic document, a specific message thread, or a booking record), send an email request to:

amanikbt1@gmail.com

Use the subject line: "Data Deletion Request"

Your email must include the following so we can verify your identity and locate the data:

We will confirm receipt within 2 business days and complete the deletion within 5 business days of verification.

4. Response Timeline & Confirmation
Service Commitment

How long does a data deletion request take?

  • In-App Self-Service: Instant — data is removed immediately upon your action within the app.
  • Email Requests — Acknowledgement: We will confirm receipt of your request within 2 business days.
  • Email Requests — Execution: Your specified data will be permanently deleted within 5 business days of identity verification.
  • Confirmation: We will send a deletion confirmation email once the requested data has been permanently erased from our systems.

Note on Backups: Deleted data is purged from live production databases immediately. It may persist in encrypted backup snapshots for up to 30 days before those backups are automatically rotated and permanently overwritten. No deleted data is ever restored from backup without explicit user consent.

5. How We Protect Your Data
Data Security

What security measures does MConnect use to protect my data?

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the MConnect app and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS. No data travels in plaintext.
  • Encrypted Password Storage: Passwords are never stored in plain text; they are cryptographically hashed using bcrypt with a unique salt.
  • Secure Authentication: Session tokens (JSON Web Tokens) are signed, time-limited, and invalidated upon account deletion or logout.
  • Zero Data Sale Policy: MConnect does not sell, rent, share, or monetize any personal user data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing platforms.
  • Minimal Data Collection: Only data strictly necessary to operate campus services (academics, rentals, communication) is collected and stored.
